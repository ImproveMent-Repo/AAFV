## 4. Exact Research Gap & Positioning Against Closest Approaches

Synthesizing the literature reveals a crisp **three-part research gap** directly addressed by AAFV-Net:

1. **Gap 1 — Representation Invariance vs. Bounded Operational Risk:** State-of-the-art AIFR methods (*Wang et al. TIFS 2025*, *OE-CNN*, *DAL*, *MTLFace*) reduce average verification error, yet output uncalibrated scores where no analytical upper bound on test-time False Accept Rate exists. Meanwhile, *Xu et al. (TIFS 2024)* and *Ahn et al. (TIFS 2024)* prove that deep matchers suffer severe calibration misalignments unless uncertainty and score calibration are modeled explicitly—yet neither provides a three-way abstention regime capable of isolating look-alike impostors while preserving zero random false accepts.
2. **Gap 2 — Unconditional Generative Aging vs. Guarded Counterfactual Evidence:** Generative aging networks (*PFA-GAN TIFS 2021*, *SAM 2021*) produce visually compelling reconstructions, yet suffer identity drift under extreme gaps or juvenile facial growth. Treating synthesized images naively creates adversarial vulnerability (echoing *Zhou et al. TIFS 2024* and *Otroshi Shahreza et al. TIFS 2024*), whereas suppressing age outright (*Razeghi et al. TIFS 2026*) discards diagnostic age-gap context.
3. **Gap 3 — Single-Metric Threshold Collapse vs. Quality-Conditioned Dual-Branch Rejection:** As *Paone et al. (TIFS 2014)* established for identical twins and our baseline evaluation confirms for Siamese CNN/ViT/Qwen2-VL, raw discriminative power (high AUC) collapses to ~0% automated accept rate under strict zero-calibration-FA rules. Bridging this requires adapting *Poh et al.'s (TIFS 2009)* quality-dependent missing-channel fusion paradigm to **direct + generative cross-age similarity**, backed by **hard guard conditions ($Q, R_{\text{sam}}, P_d, P_s$)**, **frozen SHA-256 thresholds**, and **multi-look Bonferroni zero-failure statistics**.
